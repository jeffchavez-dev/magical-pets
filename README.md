# Magical Pets

A single-player browser game designed by Mira, made with love by Daddy. Magical pets choose you, and they grow by being loved, not by fighting.

The whole game is one file: `index.html`. No build step, no dependencies (it only loads Google Fonts).

## Play locally
Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Deploy with GitHub Pages
1. Push `index.html` (and this README) to a GitHub repository.
2. In the repo go to Settings > Pages.
3. Under "Build and deployment", choose "Deploy from a branch", branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`.
4. After a minute the game is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.

## Continuing
Tap **Continue your adventure** on the title screen and you go straight back into the world where you stopped, not to the pet care screen.

## Worlds and travel
There are 25 worlds on a universe map at the start, and 25 more far worlds (50 in all) that stay hidden and uncounted until you reach level 20 (see Far worlds below). Tap "All worlds" to see them and pan or zoom around. When you go through a cave, a short animation shows you and your pets flying along the route on the universe map and through the portal.

The last 10 worlds are grim and magical, each with its own land shape (ring, crescent, islands, star, ridge, spiral, flower, peanut, comet, claw), its own music, and tougher guards.

## Danger
Each grim world has a danger rating: Low, Medium or Deadly. The rating shows under the cave name, on the universe map, and in the top bar.
- Glowing rune circles on the ground are cursed. A pet that steps on one shrinks back one level (a level 1 pet loses its progress).
- One pet type per world is safe on the curse. The game tells you which one.
- In Deadly worlds the circles are hidden until you get close.
- Tapping to walk steers around circles you can already see. Walking with the keyboard does not.
- A Ward Charm (a tool found at level 5 and above) protects your pets for 90 seconds.

## Your home world, your look, your Petdex
- At the start you choose Female or Male for the pet owner, with hair styles and outfits for each.
- After the first egg hatches you choose your home world from six looks (Sunny Meadow, Frosty Isle, Dune Isle, Coral Cove, Starlight Garden, Moonlit Glade). Every home has the lab, the sunny hill, berries, a pond and the caves.
- The Petdex lists every kind of pet you have seen, hatched or grown to level 2 or 3. Unknown pets show as dark silhouettes and fill in as you discover them.

## Pets found in each world
Every world lists the kinds of pets that live there: tap a world on the universe map (open worlds show their types, unexplored ones show `?`), tap **Pets here** in the top bar while exploring, or look at the **Found in** line under each type in the Petdex.

## Ponds, lakes and swimming
Ponds are small. Lakes (Glow Lake, Sky Lake, Prism Lake and others) are much bigger. Walk into any pond or lake and you swim. You sink down to the waist with ripples around you. Underwater you swim flat out, head first in the direction you move and leave a trail of bubbles. When you come out, whether by walking out or by coming up from a dive, you bounce out with a splash and falling drops. Only pets that can swim (water type, or fish, frog and turtle bodies, grown to level 2 or more) go in the water with you; the rest wait on the shore.

## Diving (lakes only)
When you swim in a lake, a **Dive** button appears. Dive down to a see-through underwater world and swim around by tapping or dragging. Touch things to grab them:
- **Sea friends** (rainbow fish, crab, jellyfish, octopus, seahorse, turtle, starfish). Bigger ones are worth more points: fish 3, starfish 4, crab 5, jellyfish 6, seahorse 7, turtle 8, octopus 10, and the first time you meet a kind it is worth 10 extra points and 10 coins (otherwise 2 coins). The octopus, fish, crab and starfish also give pet food (the bag limit applies).
- **A pearl.** Sometimes a pearl clam sits on the lake floor. Grab it for 30 points and a brand new water-type pet joins your team!
- **House materials** from that world (from level 3) and **star scrap** (from level 6).
- **Breath bubbles** that add 5 seconds.
- Pets that can swim dive with you. Coming up is a splash with a bounce.

**Dive score.** Everything you grab is worth points (materials 5, star scrap 8). The score shows while you dive and your best dive is saved and shown in the dive sheet.

**Lakes and ponds have different creatures.** The big lakes hold the 7 sea friends (rainbow fish, crab, jellyfish, octopus, seahorse, sea turtle, starfish) and the small ponds hold 7 different pond friends (koi, pond frog, tadpole, duckling, water snail, newt, dragonfly). Every single lake (4 sea friends each) and every single pond (3 pond friends each) in the game has its own different set, shown on the dive sheet as "is home to" with a "new!" tag for ones you have not met. Meet all 7 pond friends to become a **Pond Master** (+3 seconds of breath). All of them can live in your Aquarium.

**Sea Master.** Meet all 7 sea friends and you become a Sea Master for good: +5 seconds of breath on every dive and room for 7 air tanks instead of 5.

**Aquariums.** Every sea friend you have met can be placed in your house as a little glass aquarium (Furniture tab, 3 of the third material).

You can hold your breath for 20 seconds (4 more for each pet that can swim, up to 3), then you float back up with what you found. To stay longer, buy an **air tank** for 12 coins (up to 5 in your rack). One tank adds 15 seconds to a dive.
- **Reefs.** The lake bed has coral trees, fan corals, brain-coral mounds and tall rock spires that you cannot swim through. Steer around them. Bumping one costs 1 second of breath (once per bump), and treasure and sea friends are never hidden inside them. Sea friends swim around the reefs too.

## Flying
A fully grown (level 3) Fairy, or any fully grown bird, bat or owl, can fly when it is **first in your team line**. Press **Fly**, then tap anywhere (or on the bird's-eye map) to soar there fast. While flying, the whole team is safe from cursed rune ground. You cannot land on a glowing rune. If a flyer is in your team but not at the front, Professor Bolt offers to move it to the front, and the pet's page has a **Put in front to fly** button.

## Hunger
Pets get hungrier as they walk (and fly, a little less). Each pet has a Tummy meter on its page, and its name tag in the world says *hungry* or *starving*. A starving pet slows the whole team down. Feed pets from the bag (**Feed pets**) or at a berry bush to fill their tummies. Fully grown pets eat too.

## Controls
- **Right pad** (or arrow keys / WASD): moves your player. **Left pad** (or I, J, K, L): looks around the world. Tap the centre of the left pad (or press C) to snap the view back to you.
- You can also tap the ground to walk there, drag pets, pinch or +/- to zoom.
- The pad button in the top bar (or the P key) hides and shows both pads.

## Walk view (3D)
Open the big map (the round **Map** button) and tap **Walk view (3D)** to see the world as if you were walking it: the camera sits behind your hero, the road runs ahead to the horizon, and trees, caves, berry patches and pets get bigger as you get closer. Your pets walk ahead of you on both sides. Open the map again and tap **Back to map view** to return. The game remembers your choice on this device.
- **Move:** Up/Down arrows or W/S walk forward and back, Left/Right or A/D turn. The on-screen pad works the same way, and the right pad turns the camera. On a phone or with a mouse, tap or hold on the ground to walk there (tap the sky to turn that way, tap low on the screen to turn around and walk back).
- **Zoom** (the + and - buttons, the mouse wheel or a pinch) moves the camera closer or farther.
- Trees that stand between the camera and your hero fade so you can still see them. Danger runes show as flat glowing circles on the ground.
- Battles, caves, the Petdex and everything else work the same in both views.

**Solid objects (both views):** you and your pets bump into tree trunks, palm trunks, the body of each cave and the professor's lab, and slide around them instead of walking through. A cave can only be entered at its gate (the dark arch at the front). Flying is different: when a fully grown pet carries the team through the air, you and your pets float over trees and caves. In the walk view flying pets hover above the trees.

## The top bar
Everything is grouped into four buttons. Tap one to see its details: **World** (pets found here, danger), **Level** (abilities, unicorn rescues), **Bag** (coins, food, balls, stones, tools) and **Team** (every pet's tummy, speed). Warnings such as danger, shields or a hungry pet appear next to them.

## Powers
Every fully grown (level 3) pet has a special power that depends on its type, shown on the **Powers** button: Pixie Dust (fairy), Torch Light (fire), Pond Warp (water), Bloom (grass), Frost Calm (ice), Park Warp (earth) and Rainbow Heal (unicorn). The far-world types add Steel Plates (metal), Sunbeam (light), Dragon Roar (dragon), Lullaby (music), Sweet Treat (candy) and Star Scout (cosmic). Each recharges on its own.

**Pond Warp and Park Warp:** (no world has more than 2 ponds, 24 worlds have ponds and 23 have berry patches) with a fully grown Water pet, every pond and lake can now be dived into. With a fully grown Water pet on your team (power ready), a glowing **warp tunnel** is hidden on the bottom. Swim into it, pick one of the opened worlds that have a pond, and the water twists into a whirlpool that carries you straight into that world's pond (no splash back to land). A fully grown Earth pet does the same for berry patches (the "park"): when its power is ready, every berry patch in the world glows with a golden beacon and a **Dig & warp** label. Step on one and tap **Dig!** to go underground (a small area with boulders, roots, gems and sometimes house materials). A glowing **root tunnel** is close to the start; dig into it, pick a world, and the ground swirls you away to pop up out of that world's berry patch in a burst of petals. Each warp has a recharge time.

**Scouts:** fully grown wind, psychic, electric and shadow pets (Sky Scout, Far Sight, Lightning Runner, Shadow Scout) can be sent out to find a pet type that a guard needs and you do not have yet. They are away for 1.5 to 3 minutes of real time (you can close the game) and come back with a new friend of that type. You must keep at least one other pet with you.

Moving fast is handled by flying, so no power overlaps with it.

## World Jump
Above level 10, once every pet is fully grown, you can jump to a world 2 or 3 caves away and skip the worlds in between (only worlds you have already opened). After a jump it recharges while you walk.

## Unicorn spirits
Every 3 levels from level 10 (10, 13, 16 ... 49), a unicorn spirit starts roaming the world that you just opened (you can see it drifting near that world on the universe map). It runs and blinks around, and later spirits run faster. Like any wild pet it is only happy for a moment, so you must catch up to it and use a Happy Stone while it smiles. Once it is happy the rescue always works. A shimmering, echoing chime plays when it is near, when it blinks to a new spot, and when you open the universe map while one is roaming.

**Unicorn unlock:** at any level, a unicorn can open a guard cave for you without a battle (tap **Unicorn unlock** near a guard). Each unicorn has 2 powers; the first is free, and the second (last) one also needs at least 1 pet of a type that guard asks for on your team, otherwise it stays locked and the unicorn keeps its power. After the last one it fades away. You can carry at most 3 unicorns at a time. How tough a guard it can open depends on when you rescued it: difficulty 7 or lower before level 20, up to 9 from level 20, up to 10 from level 30 and up to 11 from level 40. Caves that need a Max Grown pet, and any guard tougher than that, must be won in battle.

## Far worlds (level 20+)
When you reach level 20, 25 more worlds appear (they are not counted before that). A cave appears at the end of each of the four old paths and leads to the Crossroads, a hub from which three caves continue. Some worlds have two caves behind one guard (beat the guard once and two worlds open). The universe map grows, and so does the list of types (metal, light, dragon, cosmic, music, candy) and the Petdex.

**Max Grown (secret, level 20+):** two fully grown pets of the same type can be merged from the Powers sheet into one Max Grown pet. Its power effects are doubled, its recharge and scouting times are halved, and each Max Grown pet adds 25% to your walking speed. The deepest caves need one. The merge plays a full-screen celebration: the two pets spiral together with a rising whoosh, flash, and the Max Grown pet bursts out with rays, rings, confetti and a chime. Tap to continue. It is much shorter and calmer for players who ask their device to reduce motion.

**Light and Dragon strike first:** if you carry a Light pet, it strikes a guard 2 times as a challenge begins; a Dragon pet strikes 3 times (the guard always keeps 1 hit for you, and the damage stays if you retry). After use, that pet is ready again once 3 more caves have been opened.

**Friendly and Normal:** the game starts in **Friendly** mode, made for young players. Guard games are slower and simpler, the guard asks for at most 5 successes, you get 5 hearts, and the difficulty never goes above 5 however high your level. There is no game over: if the hearts run out they refill and you keep going. Guards talk in their own voices, cheer you on and become your friends when you win. Open Professor Bolt's shop (tap the robot) and choose **Normal** for the original hard games, with 3 hearts, difficulty up to 16 and game over after 3 lost battles. The **Level** button lists the guard friends you have made.

**Seven kinds of guard battle, random from level 5 (the first five below, and the two gentle ones at the end):** below level 5 every guard fights with *Timing* (tap Strike in the gold zone). From level 5 up, each battle is a random pick (never the same kind twice in a row) from *Timing*, *Echo* (copy the pattern of lit pads), *Weak spots* (tap gold spots before they fade, never the red decoys), *Dodge* (move to the safe lane before the bolts land; tap a lane or use the arrow keys) and *Memory* (find every matching pair of pets), *Counting* (count the stars, later add two groups, and tap the right number) and *Friend finder* (tap every pet of the guard's type among other pets). "Try again" keeps the same kind until you win or walk away. The battle title shows its **Difficulty**: the guard's tier, plus 1 for every 5 levels above level 5 (up to 16), and speeds, windows, pattern lengths and decoys all get harder with it. Hearts, game over, Light and Dragon first strikes and the Focus Charm work in all of them.

**Free trips home:** above level 10 you get 2 free trips home from any world. Each comes back after you open 10 more new worlds. Portal uses you buy are used after the free ones.

**Tolls:** in the far worlds, guards ask for one pet at the door the first time: exchange it for a new level 1 pet from the next world, or give it up for coins.

**Game over (Normal mode only):** above level 10, losing 3 guard battles in a row ends the game. You can **rest for 3 minutes** (the guards rest too, so no new world can be opened, neither by battle nor by a unicorn, until the timer ends; worlds you have opened stay open), switch to Friendly mode, or pay coins to continue at once (30 at level 11, 5 more for each level after). A win resets the count. Starting again keeps the Petdex.

**Berries and ponds** are plentiful in the first 10 worlds and rarer after that.

## Names, home sounds and phones
The pencil beside a pet's name (on its page and in the Team sheet) renames it. On the home screen there are different sounds for petting (a chime and purr), feeding (a crunch) and playing (a boing), plus soft background music and a sound button. On phones, an eye button in the top bar cycles: show, fade or hide the top bar (the map stays). While a scout is away, a small side panel shows an animation, the pet's name, the target type and a countdown.

## Tool Cage (level 15+)
From level 15 Professor Bolt sells a Tool Cage in four sizes (Small Cage 2 pets for 40 coins, Travel Cage 4 pets for 90, Big Cage 6 pets for 160, Grand Cage 8 pets for 260). Each bigger cage replaces the one before. Open the **Team** button and tap **To cage** to move a pet into the cage, or **Take out** to bring it back. Every pet you carry slows you down, so a pet in the cage does not walk with you and does not slow you down. Caged pets do not get hungry, but their powers do not work and they do not count for what a guard asks, so take them out when you need them. Unicorns and your last pet cannot go in the cage.

## Professor Bolt's shop anywhere
Tap Professor Bolt (the robot peeking in at the corner) in any world to open his shop and buy or sell. His free colorful balls are still handed out only at his lab on Home Island. The shop has a **Tips from the Professor** button for the old help tips.


The Professor also explains the newer things. **Tips from the Professor** (in his shop, and the first-time tour) grows as you level up: at level 2 it adds diving and space, and at level 3 it adds gathering materials and building a house. The Pets sheet also has a Gathering line from level 3.
## My house (level 3+)
**How to start:** reach level 3 (open 2 worlds). Every island then has a **house plot**, a dashed house outline on the ground, and glowing **material orbs** scattered around. Collect materials by walking over the orbs. Each island gives three materials of its own (Home Island gives Wood, Stone and Straw), and winning a guard battle gives a few of the new world's materials. When you walk up to your house plot, a prompt appears with two buttons: **Build** (or **Open my house** once it is finished) and **Step inside (3D)**.

**Gathering materials:** besides the glowing orbs (now fewer, 2 per island), every material has something on the ground you work on. Tap it and your character walks over and gets to work. It takes 3 taps and a bar fills over it:
- **The real trees on the island can be chopped** (in the flat map view and the 3D walk view). Tap a tree and your character walks over and chops it. After 3 taps it falls and leaves a stump, and it grows back after about 2 minutes. Each tap shows flying bits, a ring on the ground and a floating "Chop! 1/3". Trees give the island's tree material (wood, leaf thatch, crystal, candy, ice and so on). Palm trees work too.
- Wood: chop a **tree**. Stone: smash a **boulder**. Straw: cut **tall grass**. Clay: dig a **mud patch**. Sandstone: dig a **sand pile**. Shell: pick up a **big shell**. Ice: chip an **ice chunk**. Leaf thatch: shake a **leafy bush**. Crystal: mine a **crystal**. Metal: search a **scrap pile**. Candy: pick a **candy tree**. Stardust: gather a **fallen star**.
- These things glow gold, are drawn in front of the trees, and have a generous tap area, so you can tap them even when trees are around. They also appear in open spots with few trees nearby.
- Each island has 5 of these and they are made of that island's own three materials. A used one grows back after about 2 minutes.
- **Pet helpers.** The right kind of pet on your team makes it faster (2 taps instead of 3) and can bring an extra piece: grass and earth pets for wood, earth and fire for stone, grass and wind for straw, earth and water for clay, earth and wind for sand, water for shells, fire for ice, grass and fairy for leaf thatch, earth and psychic for crystal, electric and fire for metal, fairy and light for candy, cosmic, light and psychic for stardust.

**House forms:** the house takes a different form depending on the world. Cottage (pitched roof and chimney) in meadows, gardens, reefs and windy or rainbow worlds. Dome (a stepped round roof and smoke pipe) in snowy, cloudy and deep worlds. Adobe (a flat roof with battlements and a rooftop tower) in hot, dry and ruined worlds. Spire (a tall pointed roof with a flag pole) in crystal, moon, storm and other dark or magical worlds. Worlds not listed get one of the four. The roof and the last-but-one piece are named after the form (for example Dome roof and Smoke pipe), but the eight pieces and their costs are the same everywhere.

**Building:** tap **Build** and you are taken to your 3D house, standing on the lawn as the builder. The **Build** tab lists eight pieces to build in order: Foundation, Floor, Walls, Door, Windows, Roof, Chimney and Garden. It shows a **percent built** bar, the materials you are **still missing**, and what each piece costs. You can tap a piece in the list, or tap the glowing outline of the next piece right on the house (the roof, the chimney, the flowers). Then the builder walks to that spot (and climbs onto the roof for the roof and chimney), your materials fly in, the hammer swings, the piece drops into place with a puff of dust, and you hear the hammering. A finished house plays a full-screen **HOUSE COMPLETE!** celebration.

**The 3D house:** the house stands on a little island that takes the look of the world it is built in: the world's own sea and sky colours, its ground, beach and path colours, trees in its leaf colours, how dark the world is (with stars in dark worlds), and its weather (falling snow, leaves, petals, embers, rain, bubbles, fireflies and more). It is a dollhouse you can turn around and tilt: drag the house in any direction and the view follows your cursor or finger (left and right turn it, up and down tilt it between a low side view and a view from above), or use the arrow buttons, and zoom. Unbuilt pieces show as dashed outlines. Tap **Look inside** to take the roof and front wall away. When the house is finished you can **furnish** it (bed, table, chair, rug, lamp, plant, bookshelf, toy chest, balloon, gold star): pick a piece, then tap a floor square. The builder walks across the room to that square and puts the furniture down, and walks there to put it away when you use Tidy up. The furniture is made from your materials, and **Tidy up** gives them all back. You can also let **pets move in** (2 pets, plus 1 more for each bed, up to 6). Pets living at home do not walk with you or slow you down, they wander around the rooms, and they leave small gifts of materials every few minutes.

**Teleporting:** open a finished house (tap **Open my house**) and you can teleport to any of your other finished houses. You arrive right in front of that house with your pets, and the 3D house opens with you standing inside it. Close the 3D view to walk around that island. Your materials are listed under the **Bag** button, and your houses with their percent under the **Level** button.
- **Aquarium.** Once you have met a sea friend, the Furniture tab offers an **Aquarium** (one for all sea friends, replacing the single-creature tanks; old tanks turn into aquariums with their creature inside). Tap an aquarium to choose it, then use the + and - buttons to put up to 6 sea friends inside (as many of each kind as you have met). A live picture shows them swimming, and in the 3D room they swim, turn around and crawl on the sand inside the glass.
- **Paint tab.** In the 3D house, the Paint tab lets you recolor and decorate it.
  - **House:** tap any built part (foundation, floor, walls, door, windows, roof, chimney, garden) and paint it. Pick one of 14 colors or any color you like with the rainbow circle. You can also add a design on top, in any color: stripes, dots, checks, bricks, slants, hearts, stars, waves or flowers. Whatever brush you set is what a tap paints.
  - **Furniture:** pick a piece (or tap it) and the camera zooms in on it, turning to face it. Every furniture piece is made of a few parts (a bed has a frame, a mattress and a pillow). Tap each part to paint it, step through the parts with the arrows, use Paint all parts, or Reset all. Drag to turn it around, and Back to room to zoom out.
  - **Draw freely (paint studio):** with a furniture piece zoomed in, tap **Draw freely** to open a paint studio for the chosen part. A big canvas stands on an easel, and the hero paints it: a long brush reaches from the hero to wherever you touch, wiggling while you draw. Draw on the **Top, Front or Side** of the part. Tools: Pencil, Marker, Paintbrush (thinner when you go fast), Spray, Glitter, Rainbow, Neon glow, Stamps (heart, star, flower, smiley, paw, sun, moon, bolt), Fill bucket, Text and Eraser. Pick the size with the 4 dots or the slider, any of 14 colors or any color from the rainbow circle, and for text pick the words and a font (Round, Neat, Fancy, Classic, Typewriter, Bold). **Mirror** draws everything twice, flipped, for symmetrical art. Undo, Clear, Cancel and Done. Drawings are kept in their own storage and are removed if you tidy the furniture or press Reset all.
  - Colors and designs are saved with the house. The little house picture on the world map and in the Build sheet shows the new colors too (designs only show in 3D).

## Grown-up settings (play timer)
Tap **Grown-up settings** on the title screen, or in Professor Bolt's shop (tap the robot in the world). A grown-up question (or the PIN) keeps children out. There you can choose a **play timer** (10, 15, 20, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or Off). The timer starts when the game begins, and starts again when you come back to the game after a break of 5 minutes or more. It only counts while the game is on screen, and you get a warning with 5 minutes and 1 minute left. When it runs out the game shows **Time's up!** and a grown-up must unlock it (maths question, or an optional 4-digit **PIN**) and pick 5, 15 or 30 more minutes. The lock stays even if the game is closed and reopened. The PIN and timer are stored on this device only, so they are a good guide for young children rather than a strong lock.

## Astronaut mode (level 6+)
A **Space** button is always in the walk view. Before level 6 it shows "Space (level 6)" and is locked: tapping it tells you how many more worlds to open. From level 6 it opens the Rocket station. It opens the Rocket station, where you get ready to fly to any world you have already opened. Flying skips the caves and the guards, so it never opens a locked world.

- **Star scrap.** Grey gear sparkles that appear in every world. Spend them to make the four suit parts: Helmet (4), Moon boots (5), Space suit (7) and Jetpack (10).
- **Oxygen.** Blue O2 bubbles also appear in every world (+10 each). The oxygen lives in your rocket's tank, which is much bigger than anything a suit could carry: it starts at 60 and can be upgraded with coins: Big (120), Huge (200), Giant (320). You can also fill up for 1 coin per 3 oxygen. Finishing the suit gives a free first 40.
- **Flying in space.** After liftoff you fly free on a map of space. You fly a little rocket with your face in the cockpit dome and a flame behind it, and your pets follow. Tap a world (or anywhere in space) and your ship flies there. Pick **Slow, Cruise, Fast or Turbo**: faster is quicker but burns more oxygen (Slow x0.7, Cruise x1, Fast x1.5, Turbo x2, about 1 oxygen per 70 distance). Pinch, scroll or use the + and - buttons to zoom, **All** shows every world you have opened, and **Me** follows your ship again. Drag to look around.
- **Close-up flying.** The camera starts zoomed out so you can see the whole route, then zooms in smoothly as you get near a world until it fills the screen. Your ship also slows down as it approaches. You can zoom yourself at any time, and the arrow keys (or WASD) fly the ship by hand. You never land by accident: tap Land when you are ready.
- **Real distances.** Space is 4 times bigger than the map, so the worlds are far apart and trips take noticeably longer, more so the farther the world. Oxygen costs are the same as the map distance suggests (the Rocket station lists them), and the speed and distance readouts use map units.
- **Cockpit feel.** The view is like a space game: a three-layer star field that slides as you move, long star streaks when you go fast (a blue warp glow on Turbo), a glowing engine trail, a round radar in the corner that shows every world and blinks on your target, SPEED, DIST and O2 readouts, a rotating target lock on the world you picked, and a dark cockpit edge.
- **Not enough oxygen.** If you tap a world you cannot reach, the ship does not go. It tells you how much you need, even at Slow speed. When your oxygen gets low a red button offers to fly you to the nearest world.
- **On the way.** Fly through floating O2 bubbles (+6 oxygen) and star scrap to collect them. The panel shows how much oxygen your route needs and warns you if you do not have enough.
- **The Glitch Gang.** After a while of flying through open space (away from worlds, with enough oxygen), Vex and Moxie, our own space bandits, intercept the rocket. Choose **Battle** or **Run away** (about 50-90% to escape depending on your engines, costs up to 5 oxygen, and a failed run gives them the first hit). Battles are turn-based and animated in an arena (your pets on the left, two Glitchbots on the right; pets charge up, fire bolts, orbs, falling stars or healing sparkles, and the bots lunge back). Each Glitchbot has a trick (Zapbot zaps a pet so it skips a turn, Gobbler chomps, Clanker repairs itself). Tap a pet to use its own battle power; every pet type has a different one: Flame Burst (burns), Bubble Wash (hits both), Vine Wrap (ties up), Mind Swap (dizzy), Deep Freeze (frozen bots take extra damage), Thunder Bolt (jumps to the other bot), Rock Slam, Gust Dance (dodges), Shadow Sneak (weakens), Steel Wall (shield), Sunbeam, Dragon Roar, Star Shower, Lullaby (sleep), Sweet Trap, Glitter Heal and Rainbow Heal. Stronger (older) pets hit harder and have more health. Win for star scrap, coins and +10 oxygen; lose and they take up to 5 scrap and some oxygen (your pets never get hurt for good). The gang gets a little tougher with every win.
- **Two ways to battle the Glitch Gang.** Both fill the whole screen. **Pet battle** is the close-up turn-based fight with your pets' own battle powers. **Ship battle** is a space shooter: drag to fly your rocket while it and up to 3 of your pets (as wingmen, shooting in their own colors) fire by themselves. Beat three waves of Glitchbots (zappers shoot, divers swoop at you, Clankers fire spreads), then Vex and Moxie's flying saucer, the Junk Comet. Dodge the pink shots; picking up power-ups dropped by bots gives a heart, a shield, triple shot or stars. You start with 5 hearts, plus 1 for each hull upgrade, and your engine upgrades make the rocket faster. Retreat any time (costs a little oxygen). Both battles end with the same victory celebration and rewards.
- **Winning the battle is a celebration.** The Glitchbots explode in a burst of gold with a screen flash and shake, a big glowing VICTORY! banner appears with confetti and star sparkles while your pets cheer, then a reward screen counts up your star scrap, coins and spare oxygen one by one. Bonus rows: Speedy win (4 turns or fewer), Flawless team (no pet fainted) and a Win streak bonus every 3rd win. Vex and Moxie wobble around dizzily, and every win moves you up a title: Rookie Buster, Glitch Chaser, Gang Stopper, Glitch Legend, Space Hero.
- **Underground creatures.** The Dig! scene under a berry patch has wandering creatures too: Mole, Earthworm, Shiny beetle, Fat grub, Digger ant, Cricket and Roly-poly. Every berry patch is home to its own three. Touching one meets it (+8 coins the first time, +2 after) and it is saved in your game.
- **Finding the Glitch Gang is quick.** The first interception comes after about 6 to 12 seconds of flying, later ones every 25 to 45 seconds, and a pink **Find the Glitch Gang!** button on the space card calls them right away.
- **Worlds look different in space.** Every world on the space map is a glowing planet with its own colors, its own spin speed and direction, a pulsing halo, and (depending on the world) an orbiting moon or a tilted ring. Worlds never shrink below a minimum size when you zoom out, while oxygen bubbles and star scrap stay tiny, so you can tell them apart at a glance.
- **Comets.** In open space (never near a world) a comet now and then streaks across. About 3 seconds before it arrives, a flashing red **!** marker appears at the screen edge pointing to where it is coming from, a red dashed line shows its path, and the top panel says which way ("Comet coming from the left! Steer away!"). Fly out of the way, by tapping somewhere else or with the arrow keys. If one hits you, the screen shakes and flashes red and it knocks some oxygen out of your tank (10% of the tank, at least 3, and never your last oxygen, so a comet cannot strand you). A near miss gives 1 star scrap.
- **Your rocket.** Once the suit is done, the Rocket station lets you upgrade four rocket parts, each three times (level 1 costs 6 scrap, level 2 costs 12 scrap and 25 coins, level 3 costs 20 scrap and 60 coins): **Comet shield** (comets hurt 40%, 70%, then 100% less), **Engines** (15%, 30%, 50% faster), **Oxygen recycler** (10%, 20%, 30% less oxygen used) and **Scanner** (level 1 shows comets on the radar, level 2 gives 1 more second of warning, level 3 pulls bubbles and scrap in from twice as far). The shield ring around you changes color and the engine flame grows as you upgrade.
- **Who comes along.** Pets that can fly in space (fully grown fairy, wind, cosmic, dragon and light pets, and any bird, bat or owl) fly beside you and each saves 8% oxygen. The others ride in the rocket pet pod and join you when you land.
- **Landing.** When you are close to a world, a Land button appears. You can land back where you started too. The world rises up to meet you and you touch down in a puff of dust. Flying only reaches worlds you have already opened.
- **Never stuck.** If the oxygen runs out, Professor Bolt tows you to the nearest world and gives you a little oxygen to start again.

## Weather and meteors
Every world has its own weather: snow, rain with lightning, falling leaves, floating embers, bubbles, fireflies, ash and more. Meteors fly across the universe map. Everything is turned off for players who ask their device to reduce motion.

## Prizes and tools
Beating a guard (after the very first one) gives a prize: pick coins, colorful balls or Happy Stones. From player level 5, worlds also hide a few sparkles that give small strategy tools (Joy Chime, Lucky Clover, Focus Charm, Ward Charm). Hold up to 3 of each.

## Sound
Every world has its own generated background music and ambient sounds (made live with the browser's Web Audio, no audio files). The speaker button in the top bar, or the M key, mutes it; the choice is remembered. Browsers only allow sound after a tap, so music starts when the player taps Explore the world. Turning sound on plays a short chime so you hear it at once. If the browser has not allowed sound yet, the speaker button glows with a gold ring until the first tap. On iPhone the sound uses the playback channel, so the silent switch does not block it. The little top-bar sheets (World, Level, Bag, Team) only lower the music a little.

## Phones
On Android, tapping Explore the world goes full screen and turns the phone sideways. On iPhone Safari the browser does not allow that, so use Share > Add to Home Screen: the installed app opens full screen in landscape.

Progress is saved in the player's own browser (localStorage), so each device keeps its own save.
