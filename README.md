# Magical Pets

A single-player browser game designed by Mira, made with love by Daddy. Magical pets choose you, and they grow by being loved, not by fighting.

The whole game is one file: `index.html`. No build step, no dependencies (it only loads Google Fonts).

## Play locally
Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Deploy with GitHub Pages
1. Push `index.html` (and this README) to a GitHub repository.
2. In the repo go to Settings > Pages.
3. Under "Build and deployment", choose "Deploy from a branch", branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`.
4. After a minute the game is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.

## Worlds and travel
There are 25 worlds on a universe map at the start, and 25 more far worlds (50 in all) that stay hidden and uncounted until you reach level 20 (see Far worlds below). Tap "All worlds" to see them and pan or zoom around. When you go through a cave, a short animation shows you and your pets flying along the route on the universe map and through the portal.

The last 10 worlds are grim and magical, each with its own land shape (ring, crescent, islands, star, ridge, spiral, flower, peanut, comet, claw), its own music, and tougher guards.

## Danger
Each grim world has a danger rating: Low, Medium or Deadly. The rating shows under the cave name, on the universe map, and in the top bar.
- Glowing rune circles on the ground are cursed. A pet that steps on one shrinks back one level (a level 1 pet loses its progress).
- One pet type per world is safe on the curse. The game tells you which one.
- In Deadly worlds the circles are hidden until you get close.
- Tapping to walk steers around circles you can already see. Walking with the keyboard does not.
- A Ward Charm (a tool found at level 5 and above) protects your pets for 90 seconds.

## Your home world, your look, your Petdex
- At the start you choose Female or Male for the pet owner, with hair styles and outfits for each.
- After the first egg hatches you choose your home world from six looks (Sunny Meadow, Frosty Isle, Dune Isle, Coral Cove, Starlight Garden, Moonlit Glade). Every home has the lab, the sunny hill, berries, a pond and the caves.
- The Petdex lists every kind of pet you have seen, hatched or grown to level 2 or 3. Unknown pets show as dark silhouettes and fill in as you discover them.

## Pets found in each world
Every world lists the kinds of pets that live there: tap a world on the universe map (open worlds show their types, unexplored ones show `?`), tap **Pets here** in the top bar while exploring, or look at the **Found in** line under each type in the Petdex.

## Flying
A fully grown (level 3) Fairy, or any fully grown bird, bat or owl, can fly when it is **first in your team line**. Press **Fly**, then tap anywhere (or on the bird's-eye map) to soar there fast. While flying, the whole team is safe from cursed rune ground. You cannot land on a glowing rune. If a flyer is in your team but not at the front, Professor Bolt offers to move it to the front, and the pet's page has a **Put in front to fly** button.

## Hunger
Pets get hungrier as they walk (and fly, a little less). Each pet has a Tummy meter on its page, and its name tag in the world says *hungry* or *starving*. A starving pet slows the whole team down. Feed pets from the bag (**Feed pets**) or at a berry bush to fill their tummies. Fully grown pets eat too.

## Controls
- **Right pad** (or arrow keys / WASD): moves your player. **Left pad** (or I, J, K, L): looks around the world. Tap the centre of the left pad (or press C) to snap the view back to you.
- You can also tap the ground to walk there, drag pets, pinch or +/- to zoom.
- The pad button in the top bar (or the P key) hides and shows both pads.

## Walk view (3D)
Open the big map (the round **Map** button) and tap **Walk view (3D)** to see the world as if you were walking it: the camera sits behind your hero, the road runs ahead to the horizon, and trees, caves, berry patches and pets get bigger as you get closer. Your pets walk ahead of you on both sides. Open the map again and tap **Back to map view** to return. The game remembers your choice on this device.
- **Move:** Up/Down arrows or W/S walk forward and back, Left/Right or A/D turn. The on-screen pad works the same way, and the right pad turns the camera. On a phone or with a mouse, tap or hold on the ground to walk there (tap the sky to turn that way, tap low on the screen to turn around and walk back).
- **Zoom** (the + and - buttons, the mouse wheel or a pinch) moves the camera closer or farther.
- Trees that stand between the camera and your hero fade so you can still see them. Danger runes show as flat glowing circles on the ground.
- Battles, caves, the Petdex and everything else work the same in both views.

**Solid objects (both views):** you and your pets bump into tree trunks, palm trunks, the body of each cave and the professor's lab, and slide around them instead of walking through. A cave can only be entered at its gate (the dark arch at the front). Flying is different: when a fully grown pet carries the team through the air, you and your pets float over trees and caves. In the walk view flying pets hover above the trees.

## The top bar
Everything is grouped into four buttons. Tap one to see its details: **World** (pets found here, danger), **Level** (abilities, unicorn rescues), **Bag** (coins, food, balls, stones, tools) and **Team** (every pet's tummy, speed). Warnings such as danger, shields or a hungry pet appear next to them.

## Powers
Every fully grown (level 3) pet has a special power that depends on its type, shown on the **Powers** button: Pixie Dust (fairy), Torch Light (fire), Pond Warp (water), Bloom (grass), Frost Calm (ice), Park Warp (earth) and Rainbow Heal (unicorn). The far-world types add Steel Plates (metal), Sunbeam (light), Dragon Roar (dragon), Lullaby (music), Sweet Treat (candy) and Star Scout (cosmic). Each recharges on its own.

**Pond Warp and Park Warp:** with a fully grown Water pet, stepping on any pond opens a list of opened worlds that have a pond; pick one and you warp there. A fully grown Earth pet does the same for berry patches (the "park"). Each warp has a recharge time.

**Scouts:** fully grown wind, psychic, electric and shadow pets (Sky Scout, Far Sight, Lightning Runner, Shadow Scout) can be sent out to find a pet type that a guard needs and you do not have yet. They are away for 1.5 to 3 minutes of real time (you can close the game) and come back with a new friend of that type. You must keep at least one other pet with you.

Moving fast is handled by flying, so no power overlaps with it.

## World Jump
Above level 10, once every pet is fully grown, you can jump to a world 2 or 3 caves away and skip the worlds in between (only worlds you have already opened). After a jump it recharges while you walk.

## Unicorn spirits
Every 3 levels from level 10 (10, 13, 16 ... 49), a unicorn spirit starts roaming the world that you just opened (you can see it drifting near that world on the universe map). It runs and blinks around, and later spirits run faster. Like any wild pet it is only happy for a moment, so you must catch up to it and use a Happy Stone while it smiles. Once it is happy the rescue always works. A shimmering, echoing chime plays when it is near, when it blinks to a new spot, and when you open the universe map while one is roaming.

**Unicorn unlock:** at any level, a unicorn can open a guard cave for you without a battle (tap **Unicorn unlock** near a guard). Each unicorn has 2 powers; after the last one it fades away. You can carry at most 3 unicorns at a time. A unicorn cannot open the toughest guards (difficulty above 7) or caves that need a Max Grown pet; those must be won in battle.

## Far worlds (level 20+)
When you reach level 20, 25 more worlds appear (they are not counted before that). A cave appears at the end of each of the four old paths and leads to the Crossroads, a hub from which three caves continue. Some worlds have two caves behind one guard (beat the guard once and two worlds open). The universe map grows, and so does the list of types (metal, light, dragon, cosmic, music, candy) and the Petdex.

**Max Grown (secret, level 20+):** two fully grown pets of the same type can be merged from the Powers sheet into one Max Grown pet. Its power effects are doubled, its recharge and scouting times are halved, and each Max Grown pet adds 25% to your walking speed. The deepest caves need one.

**Light and Dragon strike first:** if you carry a Light pet, it strikes a guard 2 times as a challenge begins; a Dragon pet strikes 3 times (the guard always keeps 1 hit for you, and the damage stays if you retry). After use, that pet is ready again once 3 more caves have been opened.

**Five kinds of guard battle, random from level 5:** below level 5 every guard fights with *Timing* (tap Strike in the gold zone). From level 5 up, each battle is a random pick (never the same kind twice in a row) from *Timing*, *Echo* (copy the pattern of lit pads), *Weak spots* (tap gold spots before they fade, never the red decoys), *Dodge* (move to the safe lane before the bolts land; tap a lane or use the arrow keys) and *Memory* (find every matching pair of pets). "Try again" keeps the same kind until you win or walk away. The battle title shows its **Difficulty**: the guard's tier, plus 1 for every 5 levels above level 5 (up to 16), and speeds, windows, pattern lengths and decoys all get harder with it. Hearts, game over, Light and Dragon first strikes and the Focus Charm work in all of them.

**Free trips home:** above level 10 you get 2 free trips home from any world. Each comes back after you open 10 more new worlds. Portal uses you buy are used after the free ones.

**Tolls:** in the far worlds, guards ask for one pet at the door the first time: exchange it for a new level 1 pet from the next world, or give it up for coins.

**Game over:** above level 10, losing 3 guard battles in a row ends the game, unless you pay coins to continue (30 at level 11, 5 more for each level after). A win resets the count. Starting again keeps the Petdex.

**Berries and ponds** are plentiful in the first 10 worlds and rarer after that.

## Names, home sounds and phones
The pencil beside a pet's name (on its page and in the Team sheet) renames it. On the home screen there are different sounds for petting (a chime and purr), feeding (a crunch) and playing (a boing), plus soft background music and a sound button. On phones, an eye button in the top bar cycles: show, fade or hide the top bar (the map stays). While a scout is away, a small side panel shows an animation, the pet's name, the target type and a countdown.

## Weather and meteors
Every world has its own weather: snow, rain with lightning, falling leaves, floating embers, bubbles, fireflies, ash and more. Meteors fly across the universe map. Everything is turned off for players who ask their device to reduce motion.

## Prizes and tools
Beating a guard (after the very first one) gives a prize: pick coins, colorful balls or Happy Stones. From player level 5, worlds also hide a few sparkles that give small strategy tools (Joy Chime, Lucky Clover, Focus Charm, Ward Charm). Hold up to 3 of each.

## Sound
Every world has its own generated background music and ambient sounds (made live with the browser's Web Audio, no audio files). The speaker button in the top bar, or the M key, mutes it; the choice is remembered. Browsers only allow sound after a tap, so music starts when the player taps Explore the world. Turning sound on plays a short chime so you hear it at once. If the browser has not allowed sound yet, the speaker button glows with a gold ring until the first tap. On iPhone the sound uses the playback channel, so the silent switch does not block it. The little top-bar sheets (World, Level, Bag, Team) only lower the music a little.

## Phones
On Android, tapping Explore the world goes full screen and turns the phone sideways. On iPhone Safari the browser does not allow that, so use Share > Add to Home Screen: the installed app opens full screen in landscape.

Progress is saved in the player's own browser (localStorage), so each device keeps its own save.
